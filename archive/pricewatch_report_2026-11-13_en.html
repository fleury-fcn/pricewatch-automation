Methodology

Polite HTTP collection (robots.txt, delay between requests, identified User-Agent, retries with exponential backoff). Prices are cleaned (currencies, separators, duplicates), validated against a pydantic schema and stored in SQLite through SQLAlchemy. Gap: latest price compared with the mean of the observations in the trend window. Anomaly: absolute z-score of the latest price, computed against prior history, above the threshold. Trend: slope of a linear regression over the window.
